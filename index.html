Project Communications Assistant Intern
Office of the Registrar · University of Toronto Mississauga
2024
+

The Office of the Registrar is part of the higher-education administration sector, specifically student services. Its purpose is to support undergraduate students with:

  • Enrolment & registration
  • Academic advising & degree planning
  • Financial aid, scholarships, and awards
  • Tuition and financial planning
  • Course enrolment support & program selection
  • Transcripts, documentation, and academic record management
  • Final exams & accommodations coordination
  • Convocation preparation
My Role
+

Project Communications Intern – Office of the Registrar, University of Toronto Mississauga

In this role, I operated at the intersection of research, UX, communications, and institutional systems, supporting one of UTM's most student-facing administrative units. My work focused on improving how critical academic information—registration, enrolment, tuition, and advising—is communicated to students across digital platforms.

I collaborated with communications staff, designers, and administrative stakeholders while independently owning major research and redesign initiatives. The scope of my work extended beyond execution; I was trusted to analyze existing systems, identify gaps, and propose strategic, student-centred improvements that aligned with institutional standards and branding.

What I Did?
+

Research, Strategy & Analysis

I conducted comparative research across UTM, UTSC, and UTSG websites, evaluating layout structure, navigation clarity, accessibility, tone, and consistency. This work informed a detailed evaluation of the Fall/Winter Registration Guide, where I identified strengths, usability issues, and opportunities for improvement.

I also produced in-depth critiques of key student resources, including the Program Selection Guide, Course Enrolment Workbook, and Academic Advising pages, with a strong focus on uncovering confusion points such as credit-based standing, OSAP explanations, and enrolment terminology.

This research culminated in a formal evaluation report with actionable recommendations—bridging user experience insights with institutional realities.

Web Redesign & Digital Prototyping

Building on my research, I redesigned the Fall/Winter Registration Guide as a high-fidelity Figma prototype. My redesign focused on: improving visual hierarchy and scannability; structuring content into clear, logical sections; introducing collapsible components to reduce cognitive overload; ensuring consistency with Registrar branding and accessibility standards.

I also developed a one-page referral flyer concept and a cross-campus comparison table highlighting best practices in student-facing design across U of T campuses.

Communications & Multimedia Creation

In addition to web and UX work, I supported communications initiatives by producing multimedia content for student outreach. This included: filming and editing an on-campus Program Selection Instagram reel; writing scripts, storyboards, and shot lists; editing videos with attention to pacing, transitions, and audio sync; designing a Student Testimonial Proposal, including interview questions; capturing photography at events, open houses, and student programs.

My Impact
+

My work contributed to clearer, more accessible student communication around some of the most complex and stressful parts of the academic experience. By identifying friction points and redesigning key resources, I helped surface opportunities to reduce confusion, improve navigation, and humanize institutional messaging.

Internally, my research and prototypes supported informed conversations around future improvements to Registrar-owned digital resources. Externally, my communications work helped the office better connect with students through modern, engaging formats.

What I Learned
+

This internship accelerated my personal and professional growth in meaningful ways. I gained exposure to the inner workings of student services within a large institution and learned how policy, design, and communication intersect at scale.

  • How to translate complex systems into student-friendly experiences
  • How to present and defend design decisions to diverse stakeholders
  • How to manage ambiguity, ask the right questions, and create structure where none exists
  • Leadership experience managing and delegating tasks to two interns
  • Stronger confidence in cross-functional collaboration and public speaking

Most importantly, I learned how to think strategically while executing thoughtfully, a skill I carry into every project I take on.

The Design Process
The Challenge
+

Airbnb hosts often struggle to manage properties while maintaining quality guest experiences. They need a centralized solution that handles day-to-day operations, guest communication, and service coordination without compromising their personal touch.

The Solution
+

A comprehensive platform that centralizes essential property management services, emphasizing peace of mind, efficiency, and trust. The design enables hosts to manage rentals seamlessly from anywhere while maintaining quality standards.

Global Health Core Inc.

Health Tech Startup · Selected work under NDA

The Company

Global Health Core Inc. was a health technology startup focused on improving access to high-quality healthcare through thoughtful, user-centred digital solutions. Founded in 2024, the company brings together technology, design, and accessibility to help both individuals and healthcare providers navigate complex healthcare systems more effectively. The company's work focused on creating tools that streamline operations for providers and empower users to make informed decisions about their care. Global Health Core operated with a strong commitment to accessibility, innovation, and impact—using technology not just to optimize systems, but to make healthcare more inclusive, efficient, and human-centred.

Project Overview

My Role
+

As a UX/UI Designer, I worked across marketing and product design, supporting brand growth and early product development in a fast-paced startup environment. Collaborated closely with designers and stakeholders, iterating quickly based on feedback and shifting priorities.

Challenges
+
  • Designing a healthcare platform without a medical background required translating complex information into clear, accessible, and user-friendly experiences while maintaining trust and accuracy.
  • Entering a competitive healthcare technology market required designing a platform that could clearly differentiate itself while remaining simple, trustworthy, and easy for users to navigate.
My Contributions
+
  • Designed social media assets to support product promotion and strengthen brand awareness
  • Built a mid-fidelity Figma prototype, including the Services page and an AI chatbot experience
  • Co-developed LinkedIn content alongside a graphic designer, contributing to increased post engagement
  • Adapted designs rapidly in response to real-time feedback and evolving product requirements
Skills
+
  • UX/UI Design
  • Figma
  • Wireframing & Prototyping
  • Visual Communication
  • Collaboration
  • Basic Front-End Awareness (HTML/CSS)
How has it shaped me as a designer?
+

This experience taught me that while creativity is an important part of design, understanding user needs and the context of the product is essential. Designing within the healthcare industry required a strong focus on research, clarity, accuracy, and user trust. I learned how thoughtful research directly informs stronger design decisions, and how consistency with brand identity is crucial in creating a cohesive experience. Viewing the platform from the user's perspective also introduced me to A/B testing and interpreting results, shaping how I approach usability and decision-making today.

The Daily Mail Redesign

A UX case study addressing cognitive load and navigation challenges in news media.

The Tabloid

The Daily Mail is a UK news platform that features breaking news, entertainment, lifestyle, sports content, and more. We chose to redesign the site because the original layout presents an overwhelming amount of information, frequent advertisements, and a lack of clear structure. By identifying these issues, we developed design solutions that prioritize clarity, usability, and improved content organization in the final redesign.

Project Overview

My Contributions
+
  • Designed and prototyped the sports page and site navigation
  • Collaborated with teammates on the selection of the colour palette and typography
  • Prioritized and refined advertisement placement to improve readability and user flow
  • Prototyped article layouts to support clearer content hierarchy
  • Designed and prototyped the site footer
  • Collaborated on heuristic evaluation to assess usability and identify design improvements
  • Produced the final redesign report documenting the design process and outcomes

About Me

Born and raised in Canada as a Sri Lankan creative, I've always been drawn to art and making things, even when I didn't fully know where that curiosity would lead. As digital design became more accessible, I found a space where creativity and technology could come together in a way that felt natural to me.

During my undergraduate studies, I began exploring that intersection more seriously — thinking not just about visuals, but about structure, interaction, and user experience.

I'm drawn to building thoughtful digital experiences — whether through interface design, storytelling, or developing ideas into tangible products that create real impact.

My Journey

My Story

I'm currently pursuing a double major in Communication, Culture, Information and Technology and Professional Writing at the University of Toronto, with an expected graduation in June 2026.

My journey into design began with Enactus, where I worked as a podcast editor, recording and editing interviews with small entrepreneurial businesses — an experience that later led to participation in a national competition in Toronto, where I developed confidence in public speaking in front of judges and students from across Canada.

My first internship was with the startup Global Health Core Inc., where I worked as a UX/UI Designer. This role marked my first experience designing a healthcare service portal and solidified my interest in digital design. Working in a fast-paced startup environment pushed me to think quickly, adapt, and design with real-world impact in mind.

I was involved in The DesignHub at the University of Toronto, where I created social media posts, led and participated in a case competition.

Those experiences led me to pursue an internship at the Office of the Registrar as a Project Communications Assistant, where I participated in projects such as student modules, student interviews, filmmaking, and photography. This role strengthened my ability to collaborate with stakeholders, communicate ideas clearly, and work with tools such as SharePoint in a more structured, institutional setting.
